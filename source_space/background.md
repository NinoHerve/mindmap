In this project, we model the source space using a dipole-based approach.

Brain sources are represented by electrical current dipoles (see Figure 1).

![image.png](attachment:image.png) <br>
*Figure 1: Concept of Current Dipole*

The source space is composed of numerous dipoles distributed throughout the brain (see Figure 2). In a surface-based source space, the dipoles are constrained to the cortical surface, as EEG activity is thought to originate from the post-synaptic potentials of synchronized pyramidal cells located in the cortex.

In a volumetric source space, the dipoles are arranged on a regular grid that spans the entire brain. A gray matter constraint can be applied to restrict the dipoles to the gray matter regions of the brain.
